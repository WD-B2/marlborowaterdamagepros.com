Nothing on the website should be read as an assurance of any specific outcome.

Scope and price are agreed before work starts

Before any work begins, the scope of work and the price are explained to you, so you know what will be removed, dried and repaired. Cost depends on the water category, the area affected, the materials involved, the days of drying equipment and the rebuild needed.
