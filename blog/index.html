How these guides are written

Every guide is informational. It explains how water damage works, what the common decisions are, and what questions to ask. It is not legal, insurance or medical advice, and it cannot see your house.

We leave out prices on purpose. Cost depends on the water category, the square footage affected, the materials involved, the drying days and the rebuild, and a number printed in an article is usually wrong for your property. A real figure comes from a walkthrough with a moisture meter in hand.

On insurance, the guides stay general. Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, gradual leaks often are not, and flood usually needs separate coverage. Always check your policy and ask your insurer, since only your insurer can say what applies.

When reading is not the right move

If water is spreading, close the main shutoff valve, keep people out of wet electrical areas and call (732) 352-9660. For standing water, see water extraction. For a backup from a drain or toilet, read sewage backup cleanup before touching anything.

Reading can wait while you stop the source. Wet drywall and wood do not wait long, and a day or two of dampness is often enough to turn a repair into a replacement.

Matching a guide to the stage you are in

Early on, people usually want to know what is safe to do, and the guides on assessing damage and saving photos and documents help most. A few days later the questions change to drying: is it really dry, and what happens if a wall stays damp? Weeks later, the worry is often mold and who pays for it.

For work in your own neighborhood, the service area page lists the towns around Marlboro that our crews reach. The about page explains how a call and a job work.

If you are comparing companies, the guide on questions to ask is the one to read first. It covers licensing and insurance paperwork, the written scope, and whether crews work to the published ANSI/IICRC S500 and S520 standards. Pair it with the guide on estimates so you know what a written scope should list before you sign anything.
