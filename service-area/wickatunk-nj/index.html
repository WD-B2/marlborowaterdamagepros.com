Wickatunk is a small hamlet in Marlboro Township, named on the township's history page among its older villages. It has its own post office and ZIP, 07765, but the ZIP is tiny and most homes nearby are addressed under a different one.

This page stays short on purpose. It covers the Wickatunk area only, says plainly what we could not verify, and focuses on what to tell a call-taker so the right street is found.

The Wickatunk area and its small ZIP

Route 79 is the main state road through Wickatunk, and the post office is at 248 State Route 79. ZIP 07765 is a small PO Box ZIP with 60 boxes and 145 delivery points, enclosed by 07751. The county lists its Wickatunk park under Marlboro 07746.

So this page means the Wickatunk area, not every address under 07765. Your mail may read Wickatunk, Morganville or Marlboro. Route 79 continues into Morganville, which has its own page if your street sits farther north.

What we could not verify about Wickatunk

Wickatunk is not a census place, so there are no published housing counts, home ages or flood findings for it. We will not invent them. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified, and the county hazard plan annexes name nothing specific to Wickatunk.

What does apply is general. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, and check your water bill to see who supplies your water, since that depends on the address. The township can answer either question. The page for Marlboro covers township-wide facts.

Landmarks: the county recreation area and the trail

Wickatunk Recreation Area is a 115 acre Monmouth County park with entrances at 431 Route 79 and 97 Harbor Road. The county places it on Route 79 southbound just north of Conover Road. The Henry Hudson Trail walk from Wickatunk to Matawan starts at the 431 Route 79 lot.

If you tell a call-taker the house is on Harbor Road past the park entrance, or a short way down Conover Road from Route 79, the search for the right driveway gets shorter.

Safety before cleanup

Wind can bring down wires as well as trees. On Jan 10, 2024, the township closed Route 79 between Conover and Flower Lane for trees and wires, a wind event and not flooding.

  • Stay away from downed lines and call 911 or the utility.
  • Do not run breakers while standing in water.
  • Gas smell: leave and call 911 or the gas utility.
  • Sewage and contaminated water are not a DIY job.
