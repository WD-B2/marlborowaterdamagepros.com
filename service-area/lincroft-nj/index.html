If water is still spreading in Lincroft

Take these steps in this order, and stop wherever the situation turns unsafe.

1

Find and close the main valve

In many Lincroft homes the main sits near the water meter in a basement, crawl space or utility room. Close it fully, then open a faucet on the lowest floor to relieve pressure. If you cannot find it, call the water company.

2

Cut power to wet rooms only when safe

Use the breaker panel only if you can reach it dry. A panel in a flooded basement is not safe to touch, so stay upstairs and call 911 or the utility.

3

Raise what is on the floor

Lift boxes, rugs, electronics and furniture legs off wet flooring. In a finished lower level, pull furniture a few inches from the walls.

4

Avoid dirty water

Gray or brown water from a drain, toilet or flooded yard can carry sewage. Keep children and pets out until the area has been assessed.

5

Document the water

Photograph the room, the waterline and the damaged items, and note the time you first saw water. Your insurer will ask for these.

6

Call the 24/7 line

Safety before cleanup in a Lincroft lower level

A finished basement hides outlets, a panel and appliances behind drywall, and water finds all of them.

  • Stay out of any room where water touches an outlet, panel or appliance.
  • Cut power only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. Otherwise call 911 or the electric utility before calling the restoration line.
  • Gas smell or a hissing sound: leave and call 911 or the gas utility.
  • Treat water from drains and toilets as sewage, and do not touch it.
