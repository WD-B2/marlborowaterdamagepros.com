When Texas Road is closed: access and what to tell the call-taker

The township posted Texas Road notices on three dates. On Oct 26, 2021, during a Flash Flood Warning, it listed minor flooding with the road open. On Dec 18, 2023, it reported that flooding had receded and the road was open. On Jan 10, 2024, it listed flooding near Tylers Lane between Spring Valley and Wooleytown. Public address listings place Texas Road homes under Morganville 07751.

Those notices describe those days and nothing more. Their practical lesson is access. When a road is closed, a crew may need another approach, so no arrival time can be promised. Tell the call-taker which road still reaches you, which side the driveway is on, and whether there is a gate.

Safety before cleanup

A flooded yard can carry sewage.

  • Stay out of any room where water touches an outlet, panel or appliance.
  • Do not run breakers while standing in water. Call 911 or the electric utility first.
  • Smell gas or hear hissing: leave and call 911 or the gas utility.
  • Sewage and floodwater are not a DIY job. Keep children and pets away.
